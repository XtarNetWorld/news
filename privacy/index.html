Privacy Policy

How NewsXphere handles information

Last updated: August 19, 2026

NewsXphere (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what limited data we may collect and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Basic technical data such as browser type, approximate location (via IP for weather features), and page views when you visit the site.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when you join the briefing list or contact us.
  • Local storage data for theme preference, saved stories, and reading mode (stored only on your device).

How we use information

We use technical data to keep the site working, improve performance, and deliver location-aware weather. Email addresses are used only for the optional Friday briefing. We do not sell personal data.

Third-party services

Weather features use public APIs (Open-Meteo, RainViewer, GeoJS). These services may process your approximate location. We do not control their independent privacy policies.

Cookies & local storage

We use minimal local storage for preferences. No tracking cookies for advertising are used.

Your choices

You can clear local storage at any time in your browser. You may unsubscribe from the briefing at any time using the link in each email.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Contact page.
