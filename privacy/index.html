Privacy Policy

We respect your attention and your data. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Last updated August 14, 2026 Effective immediately Operator Monu Sharma

1. Who we are

NewsXphere (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an independent publication founded, owned, and operated by Monu Sharma. Contact: team@NewsXphere.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Email address when you join the NewsXphere Briefing.
  • Messages you send via the contact form or email.
  • Stories you choose to save (stored locally in your browser).

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data (browser type, device, approximate location via IP, pages viewed) for security and performance.
  • Theme preference and reader-mode setting (stored in localStorage on your device).

We do not sell personal data. We do not run invasive advertising trackers.

3. How we use information

  • To deliver the Friday Briefing if you subscribe.
  • To respond to contact messages.
  • To improve site reliability, accessibility, and design.
  • To remember your theme and reading preferences on this device.

4. Cookies & local storage

We use essential local storage for theme, reader mode, and saved stories. These stay on your device. We may use minimal analytics cookies solely to understand aggregate traffic patterns. You can clear local data at any time via your browser settings.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only when required by law, or with trusted processors who help us operate the site (for example email delivery), under strict confidentiality.

6. Data retention

Newsletter emails are kept while your subscription is active and for a reasonable period afterward for legitimate business records. Contact messages are retained as long as needed to respond and improve the service. Local storage data remains until you clear it.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Contact us at team@NewsXphere and we will respond promptly.

8. Children

NewsXphere is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change, and continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: team@NewsXphere · ceo@NewsXphere
