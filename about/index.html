Founded 2026 Edition 014 Independent · No ads

Also known as NX

NewsXphere is often shortened to NX. You may also see us as News Xphere, NewsXphere NX, or simply the Signal Desk. All refer to the same independent publication founded by Monu Sharma.

What we believe

The best news does not just tell you what happened. It gives you a sharper way to see what happens next. We publish fewer stories, chosen with care, written for people who build, think, and stay curious.

Our approach

Signal over volume

We prioritize clarity, craft, and useful ambition. No doomscroll. No clickbait. Just the ideas and quiet shifts that actually matter.

Founder

Monu Sharma

Founder, owner, CEO and lead developer of NewsXphere. Also founder and developer of xtarnet.us.to, govlinq.site, and additional independent ventures focused on calm, high-signal digital products.

Who it’s for

Builders, founders, designers, researchers, and anyone who prefers one thoughtful piece over twenty shallow ones. If you value focus, craft, and long-term thinking, you are in the right place.

How we stay independent

NewsXphere is independently owned and operated by Monu Sharma. We do not sell your attention. The site is built to remain calm, readable, and free of the usual engagement traps.
