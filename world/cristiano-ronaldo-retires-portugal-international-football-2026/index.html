Ronaldo Hands Back the No. 7 Shirt: After 234 Games, 146 Goals and 23 Years

Cristiano Ronaldo has retired from international football. After 23 years with Portugal, he leaves as the most-capped and highest-scoring man in international football history — but the ending came after weeks of tension, not a planned farewell.

MS By Monu Sharma
• November 6, 2026 • 5 min read • World
01 Record

234 appearances and 146 goals for Portugal — both men’s international records.

02 Trophies

Euro 2016, Nations League 2019 and 2025 — three international honours.

03 Last game

24 September 2026, a 1-0 win over Wales in the Nations League.

04 What next

He continues at club level with Al-Nassr. Portugal play Denmark on 14 November.

For 23 years, the Portugal team sheet started in the same place. Cristiano Ronaldo, No. 7. On Thursday, 5 November 2026, he ended it himself, in a video message on social media. There was no final tournament, no farewell match, no lap of honour. Just a short message, and a line that will be quoted for years: the day had come to hand back the number seven shirt of the national team. It ends the longest international career men’s football has seen — and it arrived in the middle of a dispute, not as a planned farewell.

Portugal name a squad without him on Friday, 6 November — the first in 23 years.

What he actually said

Ronaldo announced the decision in a video message posted on social media on Thursday, 5 November 2026. “The day has come for me to hand back the number 7 shirt of our national team,” he said, according to reports of the message.

He went further, making clear the shirt was never his alone: “To the Portuguese people, the day has come to hand back the number seven shirt of the national team that belongs to us all. I never felt it was exclusively mine.”

He has worn the No. 7 since he was a teenager. To say it belongs to everyone was his way of handing it back without claiming it as his property. He is 41, made his Portugal debut in 2003, and retires after 23 years in the shirt.

The numbers that will stand

Ronaldo played 234 games for Portugal and scored 146 goals. Both are men’s international records — the most appearances and the most goals by any man for his country. He also recorded 37 assists for Portugal, according to USA Today.

He played at six World Cups, spanning more than two decades.

The trophies tell the other half of the story. Portugal won Euro 2016, their first major international title, with Ronaldo as captain. They then won the Nations League in 2019, and again in 2025. Three international honours in total.

His last appearance came on 24 September 2026, in a 1-0 win over Wales in the Nations League. At the time, no one knew it would be his last. It ended suddenly, in November, between international windows.

Why the ending was uneasy

The retirement followed a fallout with Portugal coach Jorge Jesus. Ronaldo was not used against Norway, and the disagreement that followed led to him leaving the camp. The Portuguese Football Federation then gave him a 30-day suspension and a fine.

The fine amount was reported differently by different outlets, so it is not stated here as a single confirmed figure. What is clear is the timeline: according to the Indian Express, citing AP, the suspension began on 1 October, which meant he had served 19 days of it by the time he announced his retirement on 5 November.

He was still serving that suspension when he announced he was done. The federation said it would not let the farewell pass “without due tribute” and would organise a tribute. The exact form has not yet been announced.

What he does next

This is not a retirement from football. Ronaldo is retiring from international football only. He continues at club level with Al-Nassr in the Saudi Pro League, where he has played since 2023. His contract there runs until June 2027.

There is also a wider hint about how much longer he may play at all. In an interview with Vogue in August 2026, he said: “This is probably my last year of football.” For now, he has only closed the Portugal chapter.

According to USA Today, Lionel Messi announced his retirement from Argentina in July 2026, after Argentina lost the 2026 World Cup final to Spain. That is reported by USA Today and has not been independently verified here. Ronaldo and Messi defined an era together, and if the USA Today report stands, they have retired from international football in the same year, months apart.

What Portugal does without him

Portugal do not have long to adjust. Their next games are Denmark in Braga on 14 November, and Wales in Cardiff three days later, on 17 November. The squad is due to be announced on Friday, 6 November, according to The National. It will be the first Portugal squad in 23 years named without Ronaldo in it.

Portugal have a younger team ready, but what changes is the presence — the captain, the record scorer, the No. 7.

Someone else will wear that shirt. On 5 November, he made it official. The shirt goes back. The records — 234 games, 146 goals, six World Cups, three trophies — stay.
