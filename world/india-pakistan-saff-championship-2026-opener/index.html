India vs Pakistan in the SAFF Opener on 5 November: Time, Venue and Where to Watch

Defending champions India begin their SAFF Championship 2026 title defence against Pakistan in the tournament opener at the National Stadium in Dhaka on Thursday 5 November. In India, the match streams on VETO — there is no television telecast.

MS By Monu Sharma
• November 5, 2026 • 5 min read • World
01 Match

Thursday 5 November 2026, National Stadium, Dhaka — the tournament opener, India vs Pakistan.

02 Kickoff

Listed as 3:00 pm IST by most outlets. One listing gave 3:30 pm — check VETO on match day (see note below).

03 Watch in India

VETO OTT platform, app and website. No TV telecast in India, per multiple outlets.

04 What next

India next play Maldives on 11 November, also 3:00 pm IST; the final is 17 November.

Some rivalries need no introduction, and India versus Pakistan in football is one of them — even if, unlike in cricket, the two teams meet less often and the stakes are regional rather than global. On Thursday 5 November 2026, that rivalry opens the 15th SAFF Championship in Dhaka, with defending champions India beginning their title defence against Pakistan in the tournament’s first match. For Indian viewers, the practical question is as important as the sporting one: this match is not on television in India. It streams, on a platform many viewers will be opening for the first time. This article gives the time, the venue, the streaming details and the tournament shape, and it flags, honestly, the two details on which outlets disagree.

The match itself

Defending champions India will begin their SAFF Championship 2026 campaign against Pakistan in their opening fixture at the National Stadium in Dhaka, Bangladesh, on Thursday 5 November 2026. The match is the tournament opener, on the first day of a competition that runs from 5 to 17 November, with six teams competing for the regional title and all matches at the National Stadium in Dhaka.

India have been drawn alongside Pakistan and Maldives in the same group, and will face Maldives in their second and final group fixture on 11 November, also listed at 3:00 pm IST. The final is scheduled for 17 November, at 7:30 pm IST according to one tournament guide. The group structure is straightforward — three teams, two group matches for India — even if, as noted below, outlets disagree on the group letter.

Where to watch in India — and why viewers are asking

In India, the match will be streamed live on the VETO OTT platform, through its website and mobile app. There will be no live television broadcast of the SAFF Championship in India, according to multiple outlets. VETO, an OTT platform by India TV, is described as subscription-free for this tournament, with the matches available without a monthly subscription or paywall as part of its sports offering.

That streaming-only arrangement is itself part of the story, because it drives the “where to watch” searches that surround a match like this. Viewers who expect to find India vs Pakistan on a sports television channel will not find it there. In Bangladesh, television coverage is on T-Sports, and in Pakistan, Myco has secured broadcast and OTT rights, according to a tournament guide — but for an Indian viewer, the route is VETO, app or website.

A note on the details that differ between outlets

Two details in this article deserve a note, because outlets do not agree on them and picking one silently would be misleading. First, the group letter: most outlets consulted call India’s group Group B, while at least one tournament guide lists India, Pakistan and Maldives in Group A. The teams in the group are not in doubt — India, Pakistan and Maldives — but the letter is, so this article names the teams and omits the letter from its main match facts.

Second, the kickoff time: most outlets list 3:00 pm IST, while one listing for the tournament gave 3:30 pm IST for India’s opener. The majority figure, 3:00 pm, is used in this article, with this note attached. Viewers should check the VETO listing on match day if they want the final, confirmed kickoff time. A single-source off-field report about India’s accommodation in Dhaka, published before the tournament, is not included in this article, because it could not be verified from a second reliable source.

None of that changes the core facts: a defending champion, a rivalry opener, a Dhaka stadium, a streaming-only audience in India, and a tournament that runs for less than two weeks. For Indian football, which rarely gets a prime-time rivalry opener, that is enough to make 5 November a day to watch — provided viewers know where to look.

Quick Answers
When is India vs Pakistan in the SAFF Championship? +

Thursday 5 November 2026, at the National Stadium in Dhaka, in the tournament opener.

Where can I watch India vs Pakistan live in India? +

On the VETO OTT platform, via its website and mobile app. There is no TV telecast in India.

What is India’s next SAFF match? +

Against Maldives on 11 November, also listed at 3:00 pm IST. The tournament final is on 17 November.
