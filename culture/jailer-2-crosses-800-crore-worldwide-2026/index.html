Jailer 2 Crosses Rs 800 Crore Worldwide: The First Tamil Film to Do It

Rajinikanth’s Jailer 2 passed Rs 800 crore worldwide on its third Sunday, 1 November 2026 — its 18th day in theatres — with an estimated gross of Rs 807.25 crore. The milestone is a gross figure, not a net one, and it makes the film the highest-grossing Tamil film of all time.

MS By Monu Sharma
• November 4, 2026 • 6 min read • Culture
01 Milestone

Rs 807.25 crore worldwide gross by end of 1 November, Day 18, per Sacnilk estimates reported by press.

02 Split

India gross Rs 533.75 crore (net Rs 456.30 crore) + overseas gross Rs 273.50 crore.

03 Speed

Rs 600 crore in 8 days; fastest Tamil film to Rs 300, 400, 500 and 600 crore, in 3, 4, 6 and 8 days.

04 Record

Highest-grossing Tamil film ever, overtaking Rajinikanth’s own 2.0 (Rs 691 crore).

There is a particular kind of box-office story that only a star of Rajinikanth’s longevity can produce: a sequel, arriving three years after the original, that does not merely succeed but rewrites the record book for an entire language industry. Jailer 2 did that on Sunday, 1 November 2026, its 18th day in theatres, when it crossed Rs 800 crore worldwide and became the first Tamil film ever to do so. The number itself needs explaining — gross, not net, estimated, not studio-audited — and the speed with which it was reached needs context. Both are in this article, because a record is only useful if you know what it actually measures.

The milestone, precisely stated

Jailer 2 crossed Rs 800 crore worldwide on its third Sunday, 1 November 2026, its 18th day in theatres. Worldwide gross by the end of that Sunday was an estimated Rs 807.25 crore, according to trade tracker Sacnilk, as reported by The Hollywood Reporter India on 2 November 2026. Gulf News reported the same milestone, describing the film as the first Tamil film to earn more than Rs 800 crore.

The breakdown matters. India gross was Rs 533.75 crore, India net was Rs 456.30 crore, and overseas gross was Rs 273.50 crore. The Rs 800 crore figure is therefore a gross figure — the total box-office value before the deductions that produce a net figure — not a net collection. Headlines that call it “Rs 800 crore earned” without that qualifier are not wrong about the milestone, but they are incomplete about what the number is. On Day 18 itself, Sunday 1 November, India net was Rs 11.20 crore across 8,906 shows, taking India net to Rs 456.30 crore.

All of these totals are Sacnilk estimates reported by press, not studio-confirmed finals from Sun Pictures. That is standard for Indian box-office reporting, where trade estimates arrive days before any official figure, if one arrives at all — but it means the Rs 807.25 crore total should be read as an estimate at a point in time, not as an audited final. No Day 19, Day 20 or 4 November total is used in this article, because none was in the verified sources read; carrying the 1 November figure forward as a 4 November total would be misleading.

The film itself

Jailer 2 is an action-comedy sequel to the 2023 film Jailer, directed by Nelson Dilipkumar and released in theatres worldwide on 15 October 2026. Rajinikanth plays Tiger Muthuvel Pandian, the retired jailer at the centre of the story. The film is produced by Kalanithi Maran under Sun Pictures, with music by Anirudh Ravichander.

The main cast named in verified reports includes Ramya Krishnan, Yogi Babu, Mohanlal, Shiva Rajkumar, Vidya Balan, S. J. Suryah, Mithun Chakraborty and Suraj Venjaramoodu, with Indian Express’s Day 13 report also listing Jackie Shroff, Mirnaa and Rithvik. Sources differ on cameo names, and this article does not present a complete definitive cast list where sources disagree. What is clear is the strategy: a sequel built around a returning star, surrounded by a multi-industry supporting cast designed to travel beyond Tamil Nadu.

How fast it got there

Speed is the second half of this record. Jailer 2 crossed Rs 600 crore worldwide in 8 days, reaching Rs 606.05 crore worldwide gross — India gross Rs 391.55 crore, net Rs 334.35 crore, overseas Rs 214.50 crore — per Sacnilk via Indian Express. It was the fastest Tamil film to Rs 300 crore (3 days), Rs 400 crore (4 days), Rs 500 crore (6 days) and Rs 600 crore (8 days), according to the Indian Express Day 13 report. By Day 13, worldwide gross was Rs 744 crore, with India gross Rs 486.50 crore and net Rs 415.65 crore.

That trajectory explains why the Rs 800 crore crossing on Day 18 felt, to trade watchers, like a matter of when rather than whether. A film that reaches Rs 600 crore in eight days and Rs 744 crore by Day 13 has already demonstrated the two things a record run needs: a huge opening and a hold. The Day 18 Sunday figure of Rs 11.20 crore net in India, across nearly 9,000 shows, shows the film was still playing widely in its third week, not merely coasting on its opening.

Gulf News also reports that Jailer 2 became the first Tamil film to cross Rs 700 crore worldwide during its second weekend, though it gives no exact date or total for that crossing in the text fetched, and this article does not invent one. The Rs 700 crore step is noted as a second-weekend milestone, without a precise day attached.

Why this record matters beyond one film

Jailer 2 is now the highest-grossing Tamil film of all time, overtaking Rajinikanth’s own 2.0 from 2018, reported at Rs 691 crore worldwide. That a star should break his own record is a neat symmetry, but the industry significance is broader: the ceiling for a Tamil film has moved from under Rs 700 crore to over Rs 800 crore in a single release, and the next Tamil blockbuster will be measured against that new ceiling, not the old one.

It also matters for what it says about the theatrical market in 2026. A film can still, in an era of streaming dominance, draw audiences to theatres in numbers that produce an Rs 800 crore gross — provided it offers a theatrical event: a returning star, a sequel audiences already care about, and a release timed to a festive period. Jailer 2, released on 15 October, had that combination. Whether that combination is replicable, or whether it is specific to Rajinikanth and this franchise, is the question producers will be asking — and the answer will not come from one film’s gross, however large.

Predictions of a Rs 1,000 crore finish have circulated, as they always do when a film crosses a round number. They are not included here as a forecast, because they are predictions, not results. What is a result, as of 1 November, is this: Rs 807.25 crore estimated gross, 18 days, first Tamil film past Rs 800 crore. That is already a record. What happens next will be measured against it.

Quick Answers
How much has Jailer 2 earned worldwide? +

An estimated Rs 807.25 crore gross by the end of 1 November 2026, Day 18 — India gross Rs 533.75 crore plus overseas gross Rs 273.50 crore. India net was Rs 456.30 crore.

Is Jailer 2 the highest-grossing Tamil film? +

Yes. It overtook Rajinikanth’s own 2.0 (2018), reported at Rs 691 crore worldwide, and is the first Tamil film to cross Rs 800 crore.

When was Jailer 2 released? +

15 October 2026, in theatres worldwide. It is directed by Nelson Dilipkumar and produced by Sun Pictures.
